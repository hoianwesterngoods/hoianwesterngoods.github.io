    Shoe weights

    Buffalo hide shoes are heavier in line with the thicknesses below. Postage uses the pair, the shoebox and the packaging weight.

    How weight is worked out

    Leather area = the outside of the bag (2 x (W x H + W x D + H x D)) plus the extra % for straps, handles and pockets. Weight = area x thickness x density, plus lining (canvas 0.35 kg/m², suede 0.45 kg/m²) and hardware. Weigh one finished bag and adjust the extra % until the number matches.

    Shipping cost by weight (VND)

    Loaded with VIO Shipping ePacket rates to the United Kingdom (from 5 Oct 2026). Shipping uses the first band the packed weight fits in. Leave "up to" empty for any weight above the others.
